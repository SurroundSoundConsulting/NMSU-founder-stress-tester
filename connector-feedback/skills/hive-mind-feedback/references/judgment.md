# Shared Judgment procedure

For new work under method 1.1, load the installed `hive-mind-judgment` skill and its `references/records.md` and `references/consumer.md` from this same combined plugin. Read `references/workflow.md` there before proposing a lesson or recovering its effects. If the skill or references are unavailable, stop new dependent work and name the missing file. These shared references own the scope, selection, proposal and confirmation procedure.

Completed historical requests keep their original read-only return; no board migration, ownership transfer or lesson generation follows from loading this revision.
